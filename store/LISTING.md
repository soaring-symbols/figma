## Name

Soaring Symbols

## Tagline

Airline logos, icons and tails as vectors: search by name, IATA or ICAO code, then click or drag.

## Description

Soaring Symbols puts a curated collection of airline logos, icons and tail designs into Figma and FigJam, as clean, editable vector layers.

**Find an airline**

- Search by name, IATA code (VN), ICAO code (HVN), callsign or slug.
- Every airline shows its codes, country and alliance; airlines that have ceased operations are marked.

**Use its marks**

- Click a logo, icon or tail to insert it. With a frame, component or section selected, it goes inside.
- Or drag it onto the canvas, or into any frame.
- Pick the size, from 16 to 256 px.
- Paint monochrome marks in any color, or in the airline's primary color in one click.
- Layers are named after the airline, like "Vietnam Airlines icon (mono)".

**Always up to date**

The marks come from the open-source Soaring Symbols collection (MIT), so new airlines and fixed SVGs show up without a plugin update.

- Website: https://soaringsymbols.org
- Source: https://github.com/soaring-symbols/figma
- Missing an airline or spotted an outdated logo? Contribute at https://github.com/soaring-symbols/soaring-symbols

Logos and icons are shown for reference. The trademarks belong to their airlines; Soaring Symbols claims no ownership of them.
